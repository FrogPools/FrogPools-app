// How it works

Browse. Stake. Launch.

FrogPools is the marketplace and launchpad for pools.trade. Discover every pool in one place, stake for a real share of swap fees, or launch your own — and the router keeps each pool getting deeper on its own.

[ 01 ]
browse

Discover pools

Browse the whole pools.trade market — trending, new and top-APR pools, with live floor, fees and volume. Find your pool in seconds.

[ 02 ]
stake

Stake & trade

Stake into any pool for a share of its swap fees in WETH, or trade in and out. Fully self-custodial — claim or exit anytime.

[ 03 ]
launch

Launch your own

Turn any pools.trade pair into a live pool in minutes. The router auto-compounds its fees back into depth — tighter prices, on its own.

// Why FrogPools

Real markets, real fees

Rewards come from actual trading, your funds stay yours, and everything settles on-chain.

[ 01 ]

Real fees, not inflation

Rewards are a share of actual swap fees — never minted tokens. When volume slows, rewards slow with it. Honest by design.

[ 02 ]

Self-custodial by default

Your staked LP stays in the on-chain program and you withdraw it yourself, anytime. The router only triggers — funds never leave the contract.

[ 03 ]

On-chain & verifiable

Every stake, fee stream and route settles on Robinhood Chain and is visible on the block explorer. Nothing off-book.
