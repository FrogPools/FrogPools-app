### 🐸 What is FrogPools?

**FrogPools** is the fast marketplace and launchpad for **pools.trade** pools. Discover every pool in
one place, stake for a real share of swap fees in WETH, trade in and out, or launch your own in minutes —
and an on-chain router keeps each pool getting deeper on its own.

> Tokens deserve a marketplace, not a spreadsheet. Trending pools, live activity, real fees, self-custodial.

### ⚡ How it works

| # | Step | What happens |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | **Discover** | Browse every pools.trade pool — trending, new, top-APR — with live fees and volume. |
| 2 | **Stake / Trade** | Stake into a pool for a proportional share of its swap fees in WETH, or trade in and out. |
| 3 | **Compound** | The router recycles accrued fees back into pool depth on a cadence or at milestones. |
| 4 | **Launch** | Turn any pair into a live, self-custodial pool in minutes. |
